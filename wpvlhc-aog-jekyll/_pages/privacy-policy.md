## Visiting this website

This website provides information about World Prayer Vision Life Healing Center AOG. It does not include visitor accounts, comments, analytics scripts, or forms that collect information on the site. Images and fonts are served with the website.

## Contacting the church

Email links open your email application. Information you choose to send, including a prayer request, is sent through your email provider to the church. Share only information you are comfortable sending by email. You can also contact us by phone.

## External services

Links to Facebook, Google Maps, and any online giving or livestream service take you to another website. Those services have their own privacy practices. The hosting provider may process connection information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure this website.
